# Menu Overhaul
Mod for the GZDoom and Raze source ports that significantly overhauls their menus.

# NOTE
As of October 15th 2025, I am discontinuing all work on Menu Overhaul. The main reason being is due to my busy life and my uninterest in the project. It got increasingly harder to focus on working on it, and I want to move on to other things.

Menu Overhaul will stay open on GitHub, and if you want to continue where I left off, you are completely free to do so. And feel free to send me your work because I will love it!
