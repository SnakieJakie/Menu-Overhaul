# Menu Overhaul
Mod for the GZDoom and Raze source ports that significantly overhauls their menus.

# NOTE
As of October 15th 2025, I am discontinuing all work in Menu Overhaul. The main reason being is due to my busy life and my uninterest in the project. It got increasingly harder to focus on working on it, and I want to move on to other things.

Another thing. In light of recent events (not to bring up outside stuff), this mod may not be really needed or warranted. UZDoom (and VKDoom) have replaced their menus with a much better version than even what this mod had, so I don't feel the need to work on MO anymore.

Menu Overhaul will stay open on GitHub, and if you want to continue where I left off, you are completely free to do so. And feel free to send me your work because I will love it!
